Tech Note: Generating Barcodes and QR Codes with 4D View Pro (TN 24-01, 4D v20, Jan 2024) — wraps 4D View Pro's SpreadJS barcode formula functions in a modular class hierarchy (_VP_Barcode_Base/_VP_Barcode_Subbase and per-format subclasses) that generates encoded images as native 4D Pictures via an offscreen View Pro area and JavaScript extraction, needing as little as one line of object-notation code.
