This Tech Note addresses the problem of users entering illegal characters into Alpha or Text fields in 4D databases. These characters—control characters, high ASCII characters, or other impermissible characters—could appear as blank spaces or be entirely invisible, making them difficult to detect. The consequences of such characters could be severe: queries and sorts might cease working properly, and indexes could become damaged. The note provides a tool for purging indexed Alpha fields used for queries and sorts. It also recommends prevention through data entry filters, providing an example filter pattern (&"a-z;A-Z;0-9; ;,;.;:;é;è;ç;ù;à;-;/;") that can be modified for specific needs.
