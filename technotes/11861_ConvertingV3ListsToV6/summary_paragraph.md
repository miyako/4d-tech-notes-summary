This Tech Note addresses a specific migration challenge: converting Version 3 lists into the hierarchical list format introduced in 4D Version 6. The conversion is limited to a single level—linked lists from v3 cannot be automatically retrieved when upgrading to v6. The note provides 4D methods to perform this conversion, helping developers who were transitioning their databases from the v3 era to the v6 platform. This was a common pain point during the mid-to-late 1990s as many 4D developers upgraded from the long-lived v3 platform to the significantly restructured v6. The full PDF was not recoverable from the original page. The techniques described are purely of archival interest today, as no practical v3-to-v6 migrations remain.
