This Tech Note demonstrates how to send email and attachments to a selection of contacts using 4D v6.5 and the Internet Commands plug-in (formerly PDM's Internet Toolkit). With this plug-in, 4D v6.5 became a powerful tool for automating email, providing both SMTP (sending) and POP3 (receiving) capabilities. The note focuses on the sending side, showing how to compose and send email with attachments programmatically. This reflected the late-1990s adoption of email automation in business database applications. Only the teaser text survives; the full PDF could not be recovered.
