## Overview
A Tech Note explaining the basics of 4th Dimension's inherited forms feature, which allows one form to reuse (inherit) objects placed on another form.

## Key Points
- Explains 4D's inherited forms feature: Form B can inherit and reuse objects from Form A.
- A form-design mechanism for consistency and reduced duplication across related forms.

## Featured Technology
- Inherited forms
- Form object reuse

Inherited forms remain a supported concept in current 4D, so the core technique documented here is still broadly relevant, even though modern 4D development (especially in Project Mode) offers additional form-reuse and componentization options beyond what was available in 4D 6.8.
