This Tech Note is a practitioner's account of migrating from Area List Pro (ALP), a third-party list display plug-in, to 4D View in existing 4D 6.8 applications. The author describes two primary ALP use cases they needed to replicate: showing a selection of records similar to an output form, and replacing included forms used to show related records on an input form. The note includes methods for both ALP and 4D View side by side, though it explicitly does not attempt to provide a complete command-by-command translation, focusing instead on a representative subset. This kind of real-world migration report was valuable to other developers facing the same plug-in transition in their own applications.
