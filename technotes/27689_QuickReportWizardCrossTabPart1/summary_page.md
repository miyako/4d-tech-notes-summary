## Overview
A Tech Note on building a custom Quick Report wizard from scratch, with a discussion of why 4D 2003's new Report editor plug-in behaves differently from traditional removable plug-ins like 4D Write.

## Key Points
- Shows how to build a quick report from scratch using a custom Quick Report wizard.
- Explains that 4D 2003's new Report editor is architecturally a plug-in, despite being non-removable and not a traditional external plug-in file.
- Part I of a two-part series, followed by a Part II focused on cross-tab reports.

## Featured Technology
- Quick Report Editor (plug-in)
- Custom report wizards

**Status:** Superseded
