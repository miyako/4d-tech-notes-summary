This Tech Note (Part 2 of a series) examines the mechanics of record loading in 4th Dimension v6.5, focusing on the implications of large records — particularly those containing BLOB (Binary Large Object) fields — on memory and network performance. In older versions of 4D, record loading was generally transparent to developers, but the introduction of BLOBs (which could store up to 2 GB of data) meant records could become very large. The note explains how 4D v6.5 handles the loading of these records, including how data is transferred between client and server. Understanding these mechanics helps developers optimize database performance by minimizing unnecessary network traffic, which is especially important in wide area network (WAN) deployments. The note builds on Part 1 of the series to provide deeper insight into record loading internals. Only the teaser text survives for this note; the full PDF could not be recovered.
