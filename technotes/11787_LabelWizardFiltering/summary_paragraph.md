Per the teaser, the Label Wizard in 4D V6 offered a simple yet powerful interface for designing and printing custom labels, name badges, or any document requiring data from many records to be printed in multiple columns. However, this flexibility introduced a developer concern: by default, end users were presented with drop-down lists of the entire database's forms and visible project methods, and a mistakenly selected form or method by a hurried or curious user could range from confusing results to serious, undesired data modifications. Even without such risks, most developers did not want end users to see their entire internal list of forms and methods. The note's stated purpose is to offer developers a way to filter these drop-down lists so that only the desired, intended selections are made available to the end user.
