This Tech Note compares two classic 4D window-opening commands, OPEN WINDOW and OPEN FORM WINDOW, explaining that while both can accomplish similar tasks, they do so in different manners, each with its own unique characteristics. It concludes that the choice between them largely comes down to developer preference, though it notes an important shared constraint: regardless of which command is used, a newly opened window must display something or be ready to accept new data, or the window will close automatically. This kind of nuanced command-comparison guidance was valuable for developers navigating 4D's window-management API.
