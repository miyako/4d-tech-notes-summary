This 2003 Tech Note, co-authored by Melinda Gallo of 4D Today and Thierry Ozil of 4D S.A., describes a PDA-style, 'click-only' interface for entering dates and times in a 4D form, building on methods from the earlier 4D Web Calendar and 4D Calendar sample databases by Steve Hussey and Dave Batton. Clicking a small calendar icon next to a date field calls CLND_DlgDay_open, which computes the click position with GET MOUSE and SCREEN COORDINATES, opens a modal popup window sized 176x206 at that location, and runs the 'CLND_CalendarSet' dialog. The CLND_Init method first detects whether the OS uses the US (MM/DD/YY) or European (DD/MM/YY) short date format by testing Day of(Date('02/01/02'))=2, then builds localized month names from a 'mois' choice list and populates the calendar grid for the current month using Add to date to compute the first/last day offsets. Clicking a month or year pop-up re-centers the grid via CLND_Calendar_Set, and clicking a specific day box (CLND_Select) updates the selected date, adjusts font style/color to mark the new selection, and calls ACCEPT to close the dialog and pass the chosen date back through a pointer parameter directly into the calling field. The time-entry widget works on the same principle with fewer permutations. The author's stated goal is simply to give users a click-based alternative to typing, not to replace typing entirely.
