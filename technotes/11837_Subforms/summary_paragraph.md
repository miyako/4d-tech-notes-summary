This Tech Note introduces subforms in 4D v6, which were previously known as 'included layouts' in earlier versions of 4D. Subforms allow embedding one form within another, enabling modular and reusable form design. The note acknowledges that subforms can be confusing for beginners and uses a real-world example to explain how and why they are used. This practical approach helps developers understand subform concepts by seeing them applied to a concrete use case rather than learning them in the abstract.
