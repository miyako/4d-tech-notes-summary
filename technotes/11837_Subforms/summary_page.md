## Historical Context
**Status:** Still Relevant

Subforms (formerly 'included layouts') were a core form composition mechanism in 4D v6. Subforms remain a fundamental feature in modern 4D, now with enhanced capabilities including list subforms, page subforms, and ORDA-driven subform data binding. The concept and usage have evolved but the foundation covered here is still relevant.

### Related Updates
- Subforms have been significantly enhanced with list/page modes, component subforms, and ORDA binding
- Subform/parent communication via CALL SUBFORM CONTAINER and form events has been modernized
