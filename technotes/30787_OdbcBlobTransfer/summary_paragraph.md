This Tech Note tackles a practical data-integrity problem when moving binary content such as pictures or plug-in documents through the ODBC protocol: such data must be transferred as a BLOB (Binary Large OBject), since BLOB is the most generic data type that ODBC-compliant applications broadly support and can hold any raw data type without altering its content. The note explains that the tricky part is ensuring that once the data is later pulled back out of the BLOB, its original data-type-specific properties (e.g., that a picture is still recognized as a picture) remain unchanged — a task requiring careful encoding/decoding technique rather than a naive raw copy. It promises to demonstrate sending and receiving data through the 4D ODBC plug-in across various scenarios.
