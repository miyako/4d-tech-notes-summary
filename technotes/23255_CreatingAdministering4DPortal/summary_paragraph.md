Written by Christian Cypert (4D & WebSTAR Plug-in Evangelist, 4D, Inc.), this Tech Note walks through creating and administering a site with 4D Portal via its browser-based Portal Administration interface at "http://127.0.0.1/portaladmin/". It covers hosting a new Portal by setting the Host Name and Administrator, choosing services (called "Portlets," such as Favorites and Reminders) to enable, setting survey question limits, date format (English vs. International), and time format (AM/PM vs. 24-hour), then submitting the configuration and signing in as the Administrator (initially the built-in Super-Admin account) from the Portal's Home Page. The bulk of the note is a hands-on tour of administering three Portlets: Forums (creating Categories that group multiple Forums, adding/removing Forums and Moderators per category, and transferring all topics/posts from one forum to another to consolidate low-traffic forums without losing data), Classified Ads (creating Categories with associated Subcategories, analogous to Forums/Categories), and Users (configuring which registration fields such as Street or Phone Number are required, and viewing/deleting/paging through the current user list alphabetically). It closes by noting these administration features let a 4D Portal operator manage its community features without further coding.
