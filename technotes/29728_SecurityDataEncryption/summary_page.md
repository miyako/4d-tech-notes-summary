## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Superseded

Data encryption remains a fundamental, still highly relevant concern for any networked application, but the specific cryptographic commands/algorithms available to 4D developers in 2003 have since been superseded by more modern, standards-based encryption support (e.g., TLS/SSL for transport security and stronger built-in encryption commands) in current 4D versions.
