## Overview
A Tech Note exploring the Postcard portlet in 4D Portal, a consumer-facing feature letting users create and share a picture-based digital postcard by email.

## Key Points
- Describes the Postcard portlet: users select a picture, add up to 200 characters of text, and the postcard is date-stamped.
- Sent either fully embedded in an email or hosted on the server with only a link emailed.

## Featured Technology
- 4D Portal
- Postcard portlet
- Email integration

## Historical Context
Part of 4D Portal's family of consumer-facing "portlets" (also including Contact Manager and Auctions portlets covered elsewhere in this batch), reflecting early-2000s web portal design trends.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D Portal has been discontinued, making the specific Postcard portlet obsolete; the general "send-a-digital-postcard" concept it implements has been largely displaced by modern social media sharing and messaging apps, so this note is now chiefly of historical interest.
