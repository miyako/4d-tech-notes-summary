## Key Points

- The note asserts that following simple backup/recovery rules and common-sense DBA practices can yield months or years of stable uptime.
- Part 2 specifically covers: the concept of 4D Backup, tips for using 4D Backup, and tips for mirroring with 4D Backup.

## Historical Context
Published March 1997, at the time 4D Server's automatic backup and mirroring functionality represented state-of-the-art reliability tooling for what was then considered "mission-critical" scale (100 users, 5 GB). Both the scale and the specific 4D Backup mechanics described are far outdated relative to modern data volumes and 4D's current backup/recovery capabilities. The archive of the full technical content could not be recovered (no working download link exists for the original page), so only the general topic and framing are documented here; the underlying discipline of planning for backup and recovery remains conceptually valid even though the specific 1997-era guidance is superseded.
