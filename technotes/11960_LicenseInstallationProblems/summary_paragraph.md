This Tech Note is a troubleshooting guide addressing the common problems 4D users and administrators encountered when installing 4D product and expansion licenses circa 2000. It covers licensing fundamentals of the era: serial numbers, and critically, the difference between the older expansion license scheme and the newer "diskless" expansion license scheme that was being rolled out at the time. Its core proposition is practical and support-oriented: help any 4D user who hits a license installation problem understand why it's happening and how to resolve or work around it, rather than simply listing error codes. The note promises a step-by-step walkthrough of installing the new diskless expansion license on both Mac and Windows platforms, reflecting 4D's ongoing effort to modernize how license authorization was delivered (moving away from requiring a physical floppy disk or dongle-like artifact toward a more software-based diskless approach). As a support/FAQ-style Tech Note rather than a development technique article, its featured technology is squarely 4D's product licensing infrastructure of that specific era rather than the 4D language or application architecture. This kind of note would have been essential reading for 4D resellers, administrators, and IT staff managing 4D Server/4D Client deployments who needed to keep licenses valid across upgrades, machine changes, or corrupted installations. Because only the teaser text survives in this archive, the specific diagnostic steps and error scenarios are not preserved here, but the scope and intent of the note are clear from its introduction and stated purpose.
