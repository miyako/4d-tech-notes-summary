## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Superseded

The Replicator library described here is a bespoke, era-specific solution that has almost certainly been superseded by 4D's own later synchronization/replication features and ORDA-based remote datastore access, though the basic principles of data replication it introduces remain foundational and still applicable today.
