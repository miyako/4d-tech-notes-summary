This Tech Note (Part 1 of a series) examines record loading mechanics in 4th Dimension v6.5. In older versions of 4D, record loading was generally transparent, but the introduction of BLOB (Binary Large Object) fields meant that individual records could become very large. The note explains why developers should understand how 4D v6.5 loads records, particularly regarding large records, memory limitations, and network traffic optimization — especially important for wide area network (WAN) deployments. Understanding these internals helps developers make informed decisions about database design and performance tuning. Only the teaser text survives; the full PDF could not be recovered.
