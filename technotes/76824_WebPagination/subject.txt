Tech Note: 4D and Web Pagination (TN 13-05, 4D v13.2, May 22, 2013) — This Tech Note surveys web pagination — the technique of splitting large result sets across multiple pages — and demonstrates four different implementation strategies for a 4D web application, ranging from client-side JavaScript-only pagination to server-managed pagination using record selections, SQL, and 4D Tags, each with documented pros and cons.
