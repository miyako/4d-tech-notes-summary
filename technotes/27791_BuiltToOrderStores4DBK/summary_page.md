## Overview
A Tech Note explaining how to implement "Built to Order" (customizable product) storefronts using 4D Business Kit, 4D's integrated e-commerce development platform.

## Key Points
- Describes 4D Business Kit as an integrated e-commerce development platform (database, web server, scripting language, multi-store management).
- Focuses specifically on "Built to Order" (configurable/customizable product) storefronts.

## Featured Technology
- 4D Business Kit
- Multi-store e-commerce management

## Historical Context
4D Business Kit was 4D's turnkey e-commerce platform of the early 2000s; only the short teaser text for this note could be recovered here.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D Business Kit has been discontinued for many years, and building a modern e-commerce storefront — built-to-order or otherwise — would today use current web frameworks, headless commerce platforms, or 4D's own modern web/ORDA/REST capabilities rather than this legacy all-in-one product, making the specific implementation obsolete though the general e-commerce concept remains evergreen.
