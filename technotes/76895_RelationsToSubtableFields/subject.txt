Tech Note: Converting relations to subtable fields (TN 13-12, 4D v13.3, November 2013) — by Milan Adamov, addresses a data-migration edge case for databases originally built in 4D version 2004 or earlier: relations pointing to subtable fields that 4D v11 SQL's converted structure silently drops, and provides a code-driven technique to restore the equivalent functionality via automated method-code rewriting after conversion.
