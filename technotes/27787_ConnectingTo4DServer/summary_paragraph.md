This Tech Note describes how the 4D Open for 4D plug-in allows a 4D Client or a standalone 4th Dimension application to connect to a specific 4D Server, and walks through the steps required to establish that connection. This is a foundational note in the 4D Open series covered in this batch, underpinning the later notes on binds, adding, updating, and deleting records remotely, since all of them presuppose a working server connection established via this mechanism. Only the short on-page teaser text could be recovered here, so the specific connection parameters and error-handling steps are not available.
