This Tech Note is Part 2 of ACI's 1998 FAQ series, following Part 1 which addressed common problems. This installment covers both additional technical issues and a broader range of questions that ACI's technical support team frequently received from 4D users. The topics likely span areas such as licensing, deployment, network configuration, feature usage, compatibility, and best practices for 4D v6 development. Together with Part 1, these FAQ notes served as a comprehensive self-service resource for the 4D user community, documenting institutional knowledge that would otherwise only be available through direct support calls. The note reflects the concerns and challenges of 4D developers working with the v6 platform on classic Mac OS and Windows in 1998.
