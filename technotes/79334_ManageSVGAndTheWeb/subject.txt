Tech Note: Managing SVG for images and the web within 4D (Revised on Nov 29th, 2023) (TN 23-21, 4D v20.2, Nov 2023) — surveys SVG's pros/cons as a resolution-independent, text-based image format for 4D forms and web areas, and demonstrates building/displaying SVG files (picture object, web area, external browsers) plus a ClassSVG example that programmatically rewrites hexadecimal colors in SVG/HTML/CSS source text.
