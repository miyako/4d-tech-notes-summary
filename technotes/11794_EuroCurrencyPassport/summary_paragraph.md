This Tech Note explains the new European monetary unit, the Euro, and how to use 4D to convert values between European currencies and the Euro. Published in June 1999, it addressed a critical business need as the Euro was being introduced across the European Union with fixed conversion rates. The note covers how to manipulate "4DK#" and "euro" resources using BLOBs, which was 4D's resource-based approach to handling currency formatting and conversion data at the time. This technique allowed developers to customize how 4D displayed and processed Euro amounts within their applications. The resource-based architecture described here (using 4DK# resources) reflects the classic Mac OS resource-fork paradigm that 4D inherited and has since moved away from entirely.
