This Tech Note is Part 1 of ACI's 1998 FAQ series, providing answers to the most common technical support questions received by ACI (the publisher of 4th Dimension, later renamed 4D SAS). This installment focuses on fixing problems that 4D users commonly encountered, likely covering issues such as installation difficulties, crashes, data corruption, printing problems, and platform-specific quirks on classic Mac OS and Windows. The note served as a self-help resource to reduce the volume of repetitive support calls by documenting known solutions. It was intended as the first of at least two parts, with Part 2 covering common questions of a more general/technical nature. As a support FAQ from 1998, it provides an interesting historical window into the day-to-day challenges faced by 4D developers and administrators of that era.
