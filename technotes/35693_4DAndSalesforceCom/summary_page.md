## Overview
salesforce merges Sales, Service, Marketing, Contracts, Document Management, and Analytics into a single suite of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) solutions.

salesforce has a unique way of looking at CRM. There is no prepackaged software with salesforce; instead, there is a fully customizable API for building software based on open standards.
