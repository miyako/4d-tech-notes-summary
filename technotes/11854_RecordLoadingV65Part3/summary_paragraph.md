This Tech Note is the third part of a series examining the mechanics of record loading in 4th Dimension v6.5. While record loading was generally taken for granted in older versions, the addition of the BLOB (Binary Large Objects) data type created scenarios where records could become very large, making loading behavior more important to understand. The note examines how 4D loads records, particularly concerning large records and memory limitations. Understanding these mechanics helps developers optimize performance by minimizing network traffic, which is especially important over wide area networks (WANs).
