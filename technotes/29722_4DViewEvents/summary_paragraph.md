This Tech Note demonstrates how the event model of 4D View, the spreadsheet-style area component, works in practice, covering the full range of supported events: On Active Cell Changed, On Cell Value Changed, On Clicked, On Double Clicked, On Drag, On Drop, On Getting Focus, On Keyboard, On Losing Focus, On Right Clicked, On Scrolled, and On Selection Change. To wire up these events, it introduces two commands: PV ON EVENT, presumably used to register/dispatch handling for the listed area events, and PV ON COMMAND, likely used to respond to standard menu/toolbar-style commands issued against the area. This gives developers the event hooks needed to build genuinely interactive spreadsheet-like behavior — validating cell edits, reacting to selection changes, or supporting drag-and-drop — inside a 4D View area, rather than treating it as a purely static display grid.
