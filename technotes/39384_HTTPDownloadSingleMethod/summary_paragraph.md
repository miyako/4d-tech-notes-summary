Written by Thomas Maul, General Manager of 4D Germany, this Tech Note packages HTTP downloading into a single reusable method, HTTP_Download, that requires only copy-pasting one method (plus the 4D Internet Commands plug-in) rather than installing several methods or a component, positioning itself as an easier alternative to the more elaborate techniques from TN 02-5 and TN 05-18 (cURL). The method takes a job type ("text", "picture", or "blob"), a result pointer, a URL, and optional Referrer, Cookie, Postdata, Timer, and Redirect parameters, and returns the HTTP response header as its function result. It automatically handles chunked transfer encoding, follows HTTP redirects (301/302/307) while preserving cookies across them, and times out after 30 seconds by default. Three helper jobs are built into the same method: URL_Encoder for percent-encoding special characters, Get_Cookie for extracting cookies from a response header, and File_name for determining a downloaded file's original name. The note candidly notes that the resulting method exceeds 500 lines — the largest the author had written in 18 years of 4D development — and explains that while large single methods are normally poor practice, consolidating already-debugged, previously-published routines into one portable method trades code-organization purity for installation simplicity across many small projects. It requires 4D 2004.1 or newer because it relies on pointers to local variables.
