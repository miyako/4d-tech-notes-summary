## Overview
A Tech Note showing how to make splitter positions in a printed output form match their on-screen positions, addressing a common form-printing quirk in classic 4D.

## Key Points
- Identifies the problem: splitters reset to default positions when a form is printed, unlike their on-screen state.
- Shows a technique to make printed splitter positions match the on-screen layout.
