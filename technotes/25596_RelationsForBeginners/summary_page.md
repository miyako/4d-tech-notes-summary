## Overview
A beginner-focused Tech Note explaining 4D's relational data modeling concepts: automatic vs. manual relations, and one-to-many, many-to-one, and many-to-many relationship types.

## Key Points
- Explains 4D's Automatic Relations vs. Manual Relations.
- Covers Many-to-One, One-to-Many, and Many-to-Many relationship concepts.
- Aimed at beginners new to 4D's relational data modeling terminology.

## Featured Technology
- 4D automatic/manual relations
- One-to-many, many-to-many relationships

## Historical Context
Reflects classic 4D's structure-defined relational model in the pre-ORDA era, when relations were managed at the Design Mode structure level or built manually in code.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Superseded

The fundamental relational concepts explained here (one-to-many, many-to-many, etc.) remain entirely valid and foundational in current 4D, though ORDA (introduced in 4D v17+) has since added a higher-level, object-based way to navigate relations that largely supersedes the older automatic/manual relation query techniques for new development, while the underlying data-modeling concepts carry over directly.
