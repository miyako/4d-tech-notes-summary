This Tech Note addresses the growing enterprise reliance on calendaring and scheduling technology, noting that broader adoption of such systems has historically been limited by a lack of Internet standards for exchanging the message content types central to these tools. It positions 4D's integration with iCal (the iCalendar standard) as a way to address that gap, letting 4D applications participate in standards-based calendar/event data exchange.
