## Overview
A Tech Note discussing how 4D can integrate with the iCalendar (iCal) standard to support standards-based calendaring and scheduling data exchange.

## Key Points
- Frames the historical limitation of calendaring/scheduling growth as a lack of standard message content formats.
- Positions iCal (iCalendar) as the relevant standard for 4D to integrate with for calendar data exchange.

## Featured Technology
- iCalendar (iCal) format
- Calendaring/scheduling standards

## Historical Context
Written when iCalendar was an emerging Internet standard for calendar data interchange, and Apple's iCal application (introduced 2002) had recently popularized it on the Mac side; only the short teaser text for this note could be recovered here.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Still Relevant

The iCalendar (.ics) format this note discusses is still a widely used, actively relevant standard today for calendar interoperability (used by Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, and others), so the core integration goal remains valid, even though the specific 4D-side implementation details from 2003 have likely been superseded by newer 4D XML/text parsing commands or third-party components.
