## Overview
The TerraServer uses standard web services to allow developers to develop web-based and non web-based application to access United States Geography Survey (USGS) imagery from their Database. TerraServer contains 3.3 terabytes of high-resolution topographic maps and aerial photos supplied by the USGS. With the high resolution topographic maps and aerial photos, users can zoom in to the pictures to display it's details or can zoom out of the picture to display a panoramic view of the image.
