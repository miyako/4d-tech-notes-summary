The TerraServer uses standard web services to allow developers to develop web-based and non web-based application to access United States Geography Survey (USGS) imagery from their Database. TerraServer contains 3.3 terabytes of high-resolution topographic maps and aerial photos supplied by the USGS. With the high resolution topographic maps and aerial photos, users can zoom in to the pictures to display it's details or can zoom out of the picture to display a panoramic view of the image. This note demonstrates consuming Microsoft's TerraServer USA SOAP web service from 4D 2004 to retrieve USGS topographic maps and aerial imagery, reflecting the mid-2000s enthusiasm for integrating 4D with external XML/SOAP web services. The Microsoft TerraServer service referenced was retired long ago, and 4D's own web-service integration approach has since shifted toward the HTTP Client commands and REST/JSON rather than the SOAP-era 4D Web Services used here, making the specific example obsolete even though the general pattern of calling external geographic web services remains relevant.
