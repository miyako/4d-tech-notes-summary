Tech Note: Replacing Interprocess Variables (TN 23-17, 4D v20, Sep 2023) — explains why deprecated, non-thread-safe interprocess ("<>") variables block preemptive execution, and walks through thread-safe replacements (Custom Constants XLIFF, shared objects/collections with Use...End use, the Storage object, on-disk files, 4D Tables, and classes) plus a practical identify/investigate/replace methodology using Find in design and regular expressions.
