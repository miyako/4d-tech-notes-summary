This Tech Note is the second in a series on networking fundamentals for 4th Dimension developers, covering LAN (Local Area Network) protocols. It addresses media access methods, transmission methods, topologies, and network devices — foundational concepts needed for smooth installation and operation of 4D products in networked environments. The note presumes familiarity with the OSI Reference Model covered in the first installment (Technical Note 99-28). Written in an era when 4D developers were often called upon to manage or troubleshoot client networks, this series provided essential background on networking principles that are now considered baseline IT knowledge.
