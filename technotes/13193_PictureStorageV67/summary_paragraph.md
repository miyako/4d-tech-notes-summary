This Tech Note by Jamras Komoncharoensiri (4D, Inc.) surveys the picture-handling commands newly introduced or renamed in 4D v6.7 -- several promoted from the old v6.5 ACI Pack plug-in into the core product, such as `PIC TO GIF` becoming `PICTURE TO GIF` -- alongside `PICTURE TO BLOB`, `BLOB TO PICTURE`, `WRITE PICTURE FILE`, `READ PICTURE FILE`, `PICTURE TYPE LIST`, and `CREATE THUMBNAIL`. It explains that QuickTime 4 or later must be installed for format-conversion commands like `WRITE PICTURE FILE`/`READ PICTURE FILE` to handle anything beyond PICT and GIF, and lists QuickTime's standard format codes (PICT, GIFf, PNGf, TIFF, BMPf, JPEG, and more). The bulk of the note walks through the "Pics Storage" example database, demonstrating picture import (single file, or an entire folder via "From Folder"), a thumbnail panel showing up to 21 images at a time with Prev/Next navigation, and a viewer window with zoom/pan tools where the picture can be moved with the `+`/`-` and `/` operators and resized with the `*` operator (fixed 25% reduction / 33.33% enlargement ratios to guarantee reversibility). It also shows exporting to PICT/JPEG/BMP/TIFF via `WRITE PICTURE FILE` or, for GIF specifically, converting with `PICTURE TO GIF` and writing the resulting BLOB with `BLOB TO DOCUMENT`, and demonstrates a clipboard round-trip (`SET PICTURE TO CLIPBOARD`/`GET PICTURE FROM CLIPBOARD`) as a trick for persisting a picture at its current on-screen display size.
