This Tech Note presents alternatives to 4th Dimension's built-in Sequence number command, exploring its limitations in certain scenarios. The note examines two possible alternative methods for generating sequential record numbers, discussing the advantages and disadvantages of each approach. It also extends one of the methods to handle the scenario of obtaining multiple sequence numbers within a single transaction, which was a common challenge in multi-user 4D Server environments. The Sequence number command, while easy to use, had limitations around concurrency, transactions, and reliability in certain edge cases that could lead to duplicate or skipped numbers. The alternatives presented gave developers more control over the numbering process, particularly in complex transactional workflows. This was an important practical concern for business applications that required reliable, gap-free sequential numbering for invoices, orders, or other business documents.
