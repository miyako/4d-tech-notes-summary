## Historical Context
**Status:** Superseded

Inter-client commands in 4D v6.5 were an early form of real-time communication between connected clients. Modern 4D provides workers, signals/semaphores, and shared objects for inter-process communication, plus the CALL WORKER command for cross-process messaging — far more robust than the v6.5 inter-client commands.

### Related Updates
- Workers and CALL WORKER replaced inter-client messaging patterns
- Shared objects and signals provide modern inter-process synchronization
