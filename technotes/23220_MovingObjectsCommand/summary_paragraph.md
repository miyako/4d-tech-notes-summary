This Tech Note demonstrates the use of the MOVE OBJECT command in conjunction with three form events — On Timer, On Load, and On Resize — showing how a developer can dynamically reposition or animate form objects in response to a form loading, being resized, or on a timed interval. This kind of dynamic object repositioning was a common technique for building responsive, adaptive form layouts in classic 4D, especially before more automatic anchoring/resizing behaviors existed for form objects.
