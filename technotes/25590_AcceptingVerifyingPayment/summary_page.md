A Tech Note discussing how to accept and verify online payment information within a 4D Business Kit e-commerce storefront, framed by early-2000s statistics on the growth of online shopping.

## Key Points
- Opens with early-2000s statistics on Internet adoption and online shopping to motivate e-commerce investment.
- Positions 4D Business Kit as a fast way to deploy an e-commerce solution.
- Focuses specifically on accepting and verifying payment information.

- Online payment acceptance/verification

## Historical Context
4D Business Kit was 4D's turnkey e-commerce platform of the early 2000s, at a time when online payment processing and PCI-style security standards were far less mature than today; only the short teaser text for this note could be recovered here.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D Business Kit has been discontinued, and payment processing has since been transformed by mature PCI-DSS compliance standards and hosted payment gateway APIs (e.g., Stripe, PayPal) that did not exist in their modern form in 2002, making the specific payment-handling techniques in this note obsolete and, in fact, likely non-compliant with modern payment security requirements.
