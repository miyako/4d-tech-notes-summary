Tech Note: Preparing External Databases for Components (TN 13-13, 4D v13.4, December 2013) — by Milan Adamov, explains how to give a 4D component its own private external database (introduced as a capability in 4D v11) rather than cluttering a host database with component-specific tables, by building the structure visually in 4D's structure editor and then converting it to a portable SQL dump that the component can use to recreate its structure and data at initialization time.
