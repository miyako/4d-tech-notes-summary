## Historical Context
UDDI was part of the early-2000s SOAP/WSDL Web Services stack intended to provide a universal service discovery registry, a vision that never achieved broad industry adoption; only the short teaser text for this note could be recovered here.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

UDDI as a technology has been effectively abandoned industry-wide — the public UDDI Business Registry was shut down years ago, and modern service discovery uses very different mechanisms (API gateways, service meshes, OpenAPI directories) — making this note's core subject matter obsolete, though the underlying idea of discoverable service registries persists in different modern forms.
