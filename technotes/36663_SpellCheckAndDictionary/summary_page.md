## Overview
4th Dimension's database contains mass amounts of critical data, codes, and rich interfaces. Oftentimes, however, you will come across misspelled words within a database which makes the database look unappealing. 4th Dimension's 2004 database has integrated a "spell check" feature that will eliminate spelling mistakes. In this technical note, I will be discussing 4D's Dictionary, how it works, and how to modify it. In addition, I will be reviewing how to synchronize multiple personal dictionaries in one.
