This Tech Note tackles the difficult problem of automatically synchronizing data between two 4D databases whose structures differ and may change over time, while also guaranteeing the transfer is completely secure. It credits the arrival of SOAP support in 4D 2003 with making this task significantly more tractable, and promises a technique that lets data be transferred without either side needing prior knowledge of the other database's structure, plus an optional fully secure transfer mode. Only the short on-page teaser text could be recovered, so the concrete SOAP message design, structure-discovery mechanism, and encryption approach are not available here. Conceptually, this note sits at the intersection of two other 2003-era themes covered elsewhere in this batch — Web Services as a new 4D integration mechanism, and data security/encryption for sensitive transfers.
