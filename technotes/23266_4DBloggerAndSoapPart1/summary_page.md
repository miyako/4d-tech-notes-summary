## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D Blogger and its SOAP-based (Blogger/MetaWeblog-API-style) architecture are long obsolete; modern blogging platforms and content APIs almost universally use REST/JSON rather than SOAP, making this note of historical interest as an early-2000s snapshot of blogging technology built on 4D.
