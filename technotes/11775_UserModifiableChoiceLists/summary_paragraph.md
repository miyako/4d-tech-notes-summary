This Tech Note addresses a common dilemma faced by 4D developers: choice lists are a popular and easy-to-implement feature (simply create a list, assign it to a field or variable, and 4D presents it during data entry), and it's not uncommon for a structure to contain 20 to 30 choice lists or more. When designing a database for end users, it's often desirable to leave many choice lists marked "User Modifiable" so users can tailor them to their evolving needs. However, because all choice lists are saved in the structure, deploying a new, improved structure would normally overwrite users' self-customized choice lists with the original default lists — leading to disappointed end users who lose their customizations. The note's proposed solution is to save the choice lists with the data instead, and to automatically rebuild the modifiable choice lists immediately upon the first launch of a newly received structure. The purpose of this Tech Note is to present a scheme for saving and maintaining user-modifiable choice lists using generic, modular code, which developers can literally copy and paste into their own structures to have a working choice-list-saving system in place within minutes.
