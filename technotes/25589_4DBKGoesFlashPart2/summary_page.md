## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

Both Macromedia Flash and 4D Business Kit are long discontinued — Flash was officially retired by Adobe at the end of 2020, and browsers no longer support it — making this note's entire technical premise obsolete; it stands today purely as a historical snapshot of early-2000s rich-client web development practices.
