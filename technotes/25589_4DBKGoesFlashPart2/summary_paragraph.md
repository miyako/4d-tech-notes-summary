This Tech Note is the second part of the "4D Business Kit goes Flash" series (Part I is also covered in this batch), extending the earlier introduction with more advanced design considerations for building a Flash-based front end for 4D Business Kit storefronts. Specific topics listed include the maximum number of characters allowed per request, how Flash interprets a space character (an easy source of subtle bugs), caching problems in Flash, automatic update mechanisms, and how to keep a user's session alive across Flash-driven interactions. These are exactly the kind of tricky, practical integration details a developer would need when using an external client-side technology (Flash) atop a server-driven e-commerce platform (4DBK).
