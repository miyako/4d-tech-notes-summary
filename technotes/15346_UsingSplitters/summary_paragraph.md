This Tech Note is a short guide to the Splitter object, a form control added in 4D v6.5 that lets end users interactively resize adjoining areas of a form by dragging a divider. The author observes that, despite being available for some time, splitters had not yet gained wide adoption among 4D developers, likely because many were unaware of the full range of interface layouts they enable — from resizable list panes to adjustable form sections. The note works through these various uses so that developers can incorporate splitters into their own interfaces. As a small, focused UI Tech Note, its featured technology is limited to the Splitter form object itself and general binary Design Mode form-layout technique, packaged with a cross-platform (Mac/Windows) example database that demonstrates the different configurations discussed.
