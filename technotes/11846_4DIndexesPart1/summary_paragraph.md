This Tech Note (Part 1 of 2) covers the type of index used by 4D — the B-tree index. It describes what a B-tree is, why it is used, how it works, and its advantages for data retrieval. Every general-purpose database engine needs the ability to search for and retrieve data quickly, and indexes serve as condensed, organized representations of data that enable fast lookups — analogous to a card catalog, telephone book, or book index. The note explains B-tree fundamentals in accessible terms for 4D developers. Part 2 was planned to cover disadvantages of B-tree indexes, 4D-specific index details, and possible alternatives.
