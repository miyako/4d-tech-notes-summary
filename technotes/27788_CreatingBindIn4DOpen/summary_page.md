## Overview
A Tech Note explaining how to create a "bind" in the 4D Open plug-in, linking data between two separate 4D systems (e.g., a 4D Server and another 4D database).

## Key Points
- Explains the concept of a "bind" in 4D Open: connecting fields/variables between two separate 4D databases/servers.
- Part of a broader series of 4D Open notes on remote database connectivity in this batch.

## Featured Technology
- 4D Open plug-in
- Field binding between 4D databases

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D Open as a distinct plug-in is a legacy product from this era; cross-database or cross-server data access in current 4D is now typically handled through ORDA remote datastores, REST APIs, or 4D's native networking commands, making the specific bind mechanism described here obsolete, though the underlying goal of connecting data across separate 4D systems remains a common architectural need.
