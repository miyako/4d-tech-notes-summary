Tech Note: Build a Multi-View Planner with SVG (TN 14-07, 4D v13.5, May 2014) — a deep, code-heavy walkthrough by Herve Le Marchand showing how to build an interactive Gantt-style multi-view scheduling planner by dynamically manipulating a large SVG document through the 4D SVG Component, applying MVC architecture, collision/overlap management, viewport zooming, gradients, and keyboard interaction.
