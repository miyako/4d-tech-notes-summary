## Overview
Part I of a Tech Note series introducing how to combine Macromedia Flash (version 4) animations with 4D Business Kit e-commerce storefronts, focusing on working around integration restrictions.

## Key Points
- Part I of the "4DBK goes Flash" series, introducing Flash-4DBK integration strategies.
- Explicitly scoped to the capabilities and limitations of Flash version 4.
- Focuses on working around restrictions when combining Flash animations with 4D Business Kit.

## Featured Technology
- 4D Business Kit
- Macromedia Flash 4

## Historical Context
Reflects the early-2000s use of Macromedia Flash 4 as a rich-client front-end technology for web e-commerce, paired with 4D Business Kit's server-side commerce engine.

## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

Both Macromedia Flash and 4D Business Kit are long discontinued — Flash was officially retired by Adobe at the end of 2020 and is no longer supported by any modern browser — making this note's entire technical premise obsolete and of historical interest only.
