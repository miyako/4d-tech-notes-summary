## Historical Commentary
**Status:** Obsolete

4D View as a distinct companion product has been discontinued and its spreadsheet-area functionality superseded by newer form objects and, for many use cases, by web-based grid components in current 4D applications, so this component-specific reference note is now of mostly historical interest. The general pattern of wrapping array/record-to-UI binding logic into a reusable helper routine remains a sound software engineering practice.
