This Tech Note explains that using a progress indicator is an efficient way to report the status of a long-running operation to the user, and describes a simple design model for building a custom progress indicator in 4D. It also includes a brief but important discussion of why displaying a progress indicator can itself reduce the total performance of the operation being monitored — the classic overhead trade-off of instrumenting a task with UI feedback.
